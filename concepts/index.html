Three new directions

Working concepts that read the live data files. Each has a day and a night look (button in the top corner, or follows your device).

A · The LedgerA broadsheet front page. Serif type, a headline written from the day's figures, a running-total chart against every year, columns for people, boats and returns. B · The BridgeA ship's-bridge instrument panel. A radar plot of the Strait with live rescue and patrol vessels, a split-flap counter, a 60-day log and a news ticker. C · TideOne idea per screen. "Calm." or the day's number in huge type over a moving swell, the year as a tide, every year since 2018 as a ridgeline, and each 100 people as a dot.
